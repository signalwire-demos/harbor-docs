## What Harbor does

You POST events to Harbor. We deliver them to your customers' HTTPS endpoints with
retries, HMAC signing, idempotency, replay, and a dead-letter queue. You don't run
the retry scheduler. You don't rotate signing keys by hand. You don't lose
deliveries when a customer's endpoint has a bad five minutes.

Three nouns explain the whole system: **destinations** (where events go),
**events** (what you send), and **deliveries** (each attempt, with status and
response). Everything else is a detail about one of those three.
